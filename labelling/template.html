How to label (read once)
  • Tick a skill if the ad asks for it from the candidate, as a requirement or a nice-to-have. Phrasing doesn't matter: "reporting dashboards" counts as data visualisation, "Kenntnisse in Statistik" as statistics.
  • Don't tick a skill that is only mentioned about the company or product, for example "we build AI products" when the ad doesn't ask the candidate for AI skills.
  • German required: tick if the ad asks for German at any working level (fluent, C1, "sehr gute Deutschkenntnisse"). A German-language ad that never states it does not count.
  • Unsure? Make your best call and write a short note. Notes help explain the results later.
  • Keys: 1–9, 0, Q–T tick boxes · → next · ← back. Your progress saves automatically in this browser.
